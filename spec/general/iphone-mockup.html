Playaway iPhone, take 2

Interactive mockup following your Spotify-style brief. HTML, not an iOS render.

  • Tap a song or the round start button to play.
  • Drag the player bar up and the card follows your pointer; let go and it settles fully open (or back closed if you barely moved it). Drag the card down the same way to close it. It never rests half open. Tapping the bar also opens it. It never closes any other way: change song, navigate or skip with it open.
  • A song with artwork opens a full card. One without opens a card that is only as tall as its rows, with the playlist still visible above.
  • Songs differ on purpose: artwork or none, artist and album or none, three cloud icons: crossed-out = this phone only, outline = in iCloud, pie = downloading. Kettle Song starts as in iCloud but not downloaded: tap it and watch the pie. Its first download fails at about 55%: the icon becomes an exclamation mark, tap it to retry (the retry succeeds).
  • The scrubber is draggable. Previous, Next, Shuffle and Repeat work.
  • The cloud icon is the control. Tap a crossed-out cloud on a playlist (Playlists screen, right side) or on a song: a dialog asks Move / Copy / Cancel (playlists: Move / Cancel). Choosing one shows the pie, then the outline cloud. Tap the pie to cancel: a sync returns to crossed-out. Tapping an outline cloud does nothing. The playlist screen itself has no playlist icon.
  • Only Morning has songs. Tap + inside it.
