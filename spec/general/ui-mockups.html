Playaway UI proposal

Left: the real playlist window today (screenshot of the running app, playing track 2 of 5). Right: the proposed layout. Mockups are HTML, drawn to show hierarchy, sizes and spacing; they are not pixel-exact AppKit renders.

1. macOS playlist window

  1. Biggest and boldest: track title (24 pt bold) and the Play/Pause circle (56 pt, the only filled accent shape).
  2. Medium: Previous/Next (40 pt targets, label colour, not accent) and the thin accent seek bar.
  3. Quiet: Shuffle, Repeat, volume, Stop and the time labels use secondary or tertiary grey. Shuffle/Repeat take the accent only while on.
  4. List: no border, no stripes; the playing row is accent-coloured and semibold with a leading marker. A selected row keeps the standard blue highlight (shown in the next frame).

2. iPhone proposal (Playaway)

Portrait, updated for the iCloud stories and the Apple HIG review (owner decisions 2026-10-13). Playback controls sit at the bottom of the screen. Play and Shuffle stay at the top of a playlist as list actions. Add Songs is a labelled button in a bottom toolbar. The sync button sits beside Edit. The accent is system blue, darkened to pass contrast; the dialog wording is illustrative, the stories own the exact text.
